WellSpace by Sereni Minds

Corporate Wellness Platform — UI/UX Prototype

A multi-tenant corporate wellness platform prototype for the Kenyan market. One codebase serves four distinct roles — Sereni Minds super-admin, client HR admin, team manager, and employee — each with its own navigation, dashboards, and workflows. Covers weekly pulse check-ins, learning modules with assessments, habit challenges, journaling, a WellPoints rewards economy, anonymized analytics, and a full content-management back office. Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 aware, with anonymized exports.

Key Features

Weekly Pulse Check-in

Four-question slide flow with emoji scales, instant personalized insights showing week-over-week deltas, and a six-week trend chart. Responses roll up into anonymized domain scores.

Emoji Scales Trend Insights

Learning & Assessments

Concept-card learning flow with read tracking, a scored assessment with per-question breakdown and explanations, badge rewards on an 80% pass, and a module completion summary.

Scored Quizzes Badges

Challenges & Rewards

Multi-day habit challenges with streaks, milestone celebrations, a department leaderboard, and a WellPoints economy with configurable platform minimums and redemption channels.

Streaks WellPoints

HR & Manager Analytics

Anonymized department drill-downs with domain breakdowns, trend charts, recommended interventions with persistent tracking, a balanced-scorecard view, and Kenya DPA-compliant report exports.

Anonymized Kenya DPA 2019

Content Management Back Office

Super-admin builders for modules, challenges, and the pulse question bank. Create, save as draft, or publish — published content persists to localStorage and surfaces in the Content Library for client HR admins to activate.

Module Builder Question Bank

Employee Self-Service

Private journaling with mood tracking, a guided breathing exercise, kudos/peer recognition, personal data export as JSON, red-zone support, and first-run onboarding with a privacy agreement.

Private Journal Data Export

Design System

Color Palette

Teal primary (#0D9488) with purple, amber, blue and coral domain accents. Full light and dark themes via CSS custom properties.

Typography

Nunito throughout, with a token-based spacing and radius scale.

Localization

Kenyan context — Swahili touches (Karibu, Asante, Hongera), KES/WellPoints, and real client organizations.

Architecture

Single self-contained HTML file. 30+ screens toggled via CSS, four role layouts, charts via Chart.js, persistence via localStorage.
