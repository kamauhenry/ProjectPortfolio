WellSpace

Corporate wellness platform by SereniMinds Wellness. UI/UX prototype

A corporate wellness platform prototype for the Kenyan market, rebuilt after a round of client feedback. Three audiences each have their own sign-in and screens: employees, the client company's Contact Person, and the SereniMinds team. Employees check in daily, work through a quarterly learning challenge, keep a private journal and use their medical cover. Companies see anonymous group totals only. All integrations (AI guidance, Health Connect, Smart insurance linking, USSD) are simulated in the browser.

Key Features

Daily Check-in & Wellness Wheel

A daily check-in of five 1 to 5 ratings and an optional typed answer, drawn from a rotating Question Bank that always includes the person's two lowest areas. Each wedge of the private wellness wheel is the 30-day average of its ratings, with a small lift for activity, across nine areas. A one-off wheel check fills it on the first day. A simulated AI reads the typed answer for tone and themes and shows a support line when an entry is worrying.

1 to 5 Ratings Wellness Wheel

Quarterly Challenge

Learning is organised as a Quarterly Challenge with one Topic a month and one Bite a week. Each Bite opens its own screen with a reading and discussion, an optional video and scored questions. Short optional Extra Challenges run alongside.

Topics & Bites Quiz Questions

Leaderboard & Rewards

An individual leaderboard with an opt-out, milestone awards and WellPoints earned only from activity, never from how someone feels. Points can be spent on insurer-funded rewards such as a pharmacy voucher.

WellPoints Opt-out

Support & Medical Cover

A support screen with a toll-free line, counsellor booking and the help available for each area. A cover screen shows a digital card, outpatient balance, places that accept it and a pharmacy order paid from cover. A USSD flow offers the check-in to feature phones. Employees can register dependants, and see company wellness events and say they will attend.

Counsellor Booking USSD

Anonymous Company Reports

The Contact Person sees reports under eight headings, grouped by age band, gender, location, tenure, work pattern or engagement. Groups under five people are hidden, and care figures are never broken down by group.

Small-group Protection Kenya DPA 2019

SereniMinds Back Office

Company onboarding with a checklist that gates the programme, a company page with billing and an activity log, a challenge builder with a live phone preview and AI suggestions, a Question Bank with approval of AI drafts, camps and meet-ups planned in five steps, a rewards catalogue with configurable points, and insurance partners.

Challenge Builder Question Bank

Design System

Color Palette

Teal primary (#0D9488) with purple, amber, blue and coral domain accents. Full light and dark themes via CSS custom properties.

Typography

Nunito throughout, with a token-based spacing and radius scale.

Localization

Kenyan context: a Kiswahili option (beta) for the main employee screens, KES and WellPoints, and sample Kenyan organisations.

Architecture

Single self-contained HTML file. Screens are rendered from plain data objects by small template functions, with one localStorage key for saved state and the simulated AI kept in a separate set of pure functions.
