Sereniminds Wellness Clinic

Clinic Management System — UI/UX Prototype

A comprehensive clinic management system prototype for a mental health & psychology clinic in Nairobi, Kenya. Designed to handle the full clinic workflow: appointment scheduling across 2 consultation rooms, clinical documentation (EHR/SOAP notes), billing with KRA eTIMS fiscalization, M-PESA payments, petty cash management, consultant payments, and TCA/follow-up tracking — 42 fully interactive views.

Key Features

Appointment Scheduling

Day view with Room A / Room B timeline columns inspired by surgery schedule design. 60-minute sessions with 15-minute buffers. Floating room model — any clinician, any room, with conflict detection.

Dual-Room 75-min Slots

Clinical Documentation (EHR)

SOAP-style note editor with DSM-5/ICD-10 diagnosis lookup, consent status tracking per-note per-clinician, progress notes, treatment plans, outcome measures, and supportive clinical data uploads.

SOAP Notes DSM-5 / ICD-10

Billing & Fiscalization

Full billing lifecycle: M-PESA PayBill → payment callback → invoice marked paid → KRA eTIMS OSCU real-time submission → ETR receipt generated. Consultant payments with eTIMS invoices. Petty cash with two-step approval workflow.

eTIMS M-PESA KES Currency

Client Portal

Complete client-facing experience: secure login, 3-step intake wizard, appointment booking, payment history, profile management. Paperless registration with all background data collected online.

Intake Wizard Self-Service

EAP & Training Management

Employee Assistance Program organization tracking with detailed org profiles (Overview, Employees, Sessions, Reports tabs). Workshop/program training management with filter chips and expandable rows.

EAP Training Tracking

Financial Reporting

Complete financial suite: Balance Sheet, Income Statement (P&L), General Ledger, Trial Balance, Payroll, Debtors & Creditors registers. All denominated in KES with eTIMS receipt references.

6 Financial Views KRA Compliant
