Key Features

Group Buying Engine

Customers browse MOQ campaigns, join existing group buys, or request new ones. MOQ progress is tracked per product — each purchase moves the group closer to the import threshold, unlocking wholesale pricing for everyone.

MOQ Tracking Campaign System

M-Pesa Mobile Payments

Integrated Daraja API for STK Push payments. Customers pay via M-Pesa directly on the platform. Payment confirmation is automatic — no manual reconciliation. Supports both Pay & Pick instant purchases and MOQ campaign pledges.

STK Push Auto-Confirm

Comprehensive product catalog: Baby/Kids & Games, Bags & Footwear, Beauty/Personal/Health, Clothes & Accessories, Electronics, Home & Kitchen, IKEA, Machine & Industrials, Office, Perfumes & Fragrances, Sports/Outdoor/Entertainment, Swag/Branded Gift Items.

Business Model

Pay & Pick

Instant purchase at wholesale prices — no waiting for group fills. Ideal for single buyers who need items immediately.

MOQ Campaigns

Join existing group buys to unlock the lowest wholesale prices. Pool orders with other importers to meet supplier minimums.

Request Campaign

Don't see what you need? Request a new MOQ campaign. Mustard sources it from their supplier network and opens it for group buying.

Revenue Model

Live business generating revenue through wholesale markups, MOQ campaign fills, and premium placement for featured products.
